THINK BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

Ask yourself five simple questions.

These questions can help you slow down, consider the situation, and make a more informed decision.

QUESTION 01

Do I understand what I am being asked to do?

QUESTION 02

What could happen as a result of this choice?

QUESTION 03

Am I making this decision freely or because of pressure?

QUESTION 04

Is the information I received reliable?

QUESTION 05

Who could I talk to if I need support?
