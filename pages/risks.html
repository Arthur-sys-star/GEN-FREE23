Know the Risks.

Understanding the risks of drug abuse is the first step toward making informed and healthier choices.

RISK 01
WHY IT MATTERS

Drug abuse can affect more than one part of life.

The effects can influence physical health, mental well-being, relationships, education and future opportunities.
