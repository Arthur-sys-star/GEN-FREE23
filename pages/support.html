SUPPORT • LISTEN • ACT

Asking for help is a strength.

You don't have to handle every difficult situation alone. Reaching out to someone you trust can be an important step toward making a healthier choice.

YOU ARE NOT
ALONE REACH OUT
KNOW WHEN TO REACH OUT

Support can start with one conversation.

If you are worried about substance use, feeling pressured, or concerned about someone you know, talking to a trusted person can help you take the next step.

01
START WITH TRUST

Talk to someone you trust

This could be a parent, teacher, college mentor, counsellor, close friend, or another responsible person who can listen without judging you.

02
DON'T FACE IT ALONE

Ask for professional support

If the situation feels difficult to manage, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional or appropriate support service.

03
SUPPORT OTHERS

Listen without judging

If someone comes to you for help, listen calmly, avoid shaming them, and encourage them to speak with a trusted adult or qualified professional.

04
PRIORITIZE SAFETY

Get urgent help when necessary

If someone is in immediate danger or experiencing a serious medical emergency, seek emergency medical assistance immediately.

NEED HELP NOW

Talk to a trained person, today.

These free services are for anyone worried about substance use, including family and friends. You do not need to be in crisis to call.

DRUG DE-ADDICTION
14446

National de-addiction helpline

Toll-free primary counselling and referral for people affected by substance use and their families (Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan).

Call 14446
MENTAL HEALTH
14416

Tele-MANAS

24×7 toll-free mental-health counselling in multiple languages, useful for stress, pressure or low mood.

Call 14416
EMERGENCY
112

Immediate danger

If someone is unconscious, having trouble breathing, or in immediate danger, call the emergency number straight away.

Call 112

These numbers are for India and are free to call.
