QUESTION • CHECK • UNDERSTAND

Not everything you hear is true.

Myths about drugs and substance use can influence decisions. Learn to question claims and look for reliable information.

MYTH VS FACT
THINK BEFORE YOU BELIEVE

A claim isn't automatically true because everyone says it.

Let's examine some common beliefs and compare them with evidence-based awareness information.

01
MYTH

“Trying a drug once can't really hurt.”

A single experience can still involve risks, including unexpected reactions, impaired judgement or unsafe situations.

VS
FACT

One decision can carry real risks.

The effects of a substance can vary between people, and there is no guarantee that an experience will be harmless.

02
MYTH

“Everyone is doing it.”

Social pressure can make substance use appear more common than it actually is.

VS
FACT

You don't have to follow the crowd.

Your choices should be based on your own values, safety and understanding—not pressure.

03
MYTH

“Asking for help means you're weak.”

Some people avoid support because they fear being judged by others.

VS
FACT

Reaching out is a strength.

Talking to someone you trust can be an important step toward making safer and healthier choices.

03
GEN-FREE MINDSET

Question the claim. Check the facts.

Being informed helps you make decisions based on knowledge rather than rumours, pressure or assumptions.
